1. Project Overview  
Rownaa is a car rental marketplace website, structured similarly to ride-sharing platforms (e.g. Pathao, Uber) but 
built around scheduled rentals rather than point-to-point pickups. 
The platform owner does not own any vehicles. Car owners and drivers register on the platform and list their 
vehicles. Customers browse and book based on pickup location, date, and service type. The platform takes a 
commission on each completed booking. 
1.1 Business Model  
• Marketplace model: car owners/drivers register and list vehicles; the platform does not own any cars 
• Commission: approximately 10% per booking, taken from the total fare 
• Payment split: customer pays a 20% advance online to confirm the booking; the remaining balance is paid 
directly to the driver in person 
• Owners/drivers are verified (documents checked) before being approved to receive bookings 
• Initial services offered: Airport Transfer, Corporate Transport, Car Rental with Hourly, Intercity Travel, Travel 
& Tour, Event & Wedding Transport. 
1.2 User Roles  
• Customer — books a car for a service 
• Car Owner / Driver — registers a vehicle (and optionally a driver) and fulfills bookings 
• Admin — verifies users, oversees bookings, tracks commission, manages disputes and platform content 
2. Customer Panel — Booking Flow  
The customer selects one of 6 services on the homepage, then a service-wise booking form appears. 
2.1 Common Fields (present on every service)  
• Name 
• Mobile number 
• Pickup location (address / area select — map picker preferred) 
• Pickup date 
• Pickup time 
• Car type preference (Sedan / SUV / Microbus / Noah-Hiace / Any) 
• Special note / instruction (optional text box) 
• Advance payment (20%) — via bKash / Bank Transfer or SSLCommerz at confirmation

ROWNAA — Developer Specification (Master Edition) Page 3 of 8  2.3 Seat Matching Logic  
Based on how many passengers the trip creator wants to carry, the system should automatically filter and suggest 
available cars matching that count. 
The system should display: 
• Total passengers required 
• How many cars are needed 
• Which car type(s) are needed 
• Available seats per car 
• Combined total seats across the suggested cars 
Example — 10 passengers:  
• Sedan — 4 seats 
• SUV — 6 seats 
• Result: 2 cars = 10 seats total  
2.4 Customer Panel — Feature List  
• Sign up / login (phone number or email) 
• Homepage with the 6 service options 
• Automatic filtering: only cars registered near the selected pickup area are shown 
• Car listing view: photos, model, seat capacity, rate, driver rating 
• Booking confirmation with online advance payment 
• “My Bookings” page showing status: Pending / Confirmed / Ongoing / Completed / Cancelled 
• Option to rate and review the driver/car after a completed trip 
⚠ Developer Notes — Booking Data  
• Every service form submission creates a booking request in the backend containing: service type, all field data, 
customer info, and status (pending / confirmed / completed).  
• Keep the pickup location field structured (dropdown or map-based area select), not free-text — this is 
required to filter and match the nearest registered car owner/driver.  
• Intercity Travel is a standalone service, separate from Travel & Tour — Intercity is for straightforward point-
to-point city-to-city trips, while Travel & Tour covers multi-destination/multi-day tour packages with an 
itinerary.

Step-by-step registration flow for car owners and rental businesses.

• Manufacturing year (Optional) 
• Seat count 
• Car color 
• Number plate 
• Car photos (front, back, inside — a few) 
• Registration paper (BRTA) upload 
• Fitness certificate upload (Expire date entry must) 
• Tax token upload 
• Insurance paper (if available) 
Step 4 — Driver Information (if owner keeps a driver instead of driving)  
• Driver's name 
• Phone number 
• Driving license (photo upload, front-back) 
• Driver's photo 
• Experience (years, optional) 
If the owner is the driver themself, this step is skipped.  
Step 5 — Service Preference  
• Which services they want to offer (checkbox: Airport Transfer / Corporate / Rental / Intercity / Tour / Event) 
(Choose any 3 options) 
• Availability: Full-time / Part-time / Weekend only 
• Operating area (select one or more areas) 
Step 6 — Payment / Bank Info  
• bKash/Nagad number (for receiving payouts) 
• Bank account (optional, if bank transfer is kept)

4. Admin Panel — Full Structure  
Complete panel-by-panel design for the Rownaa admin — covering owner registration, bookings, drivers, 
payments, and platform control. 
Panel 1 — Dashboard (Overview)  
• Total bookings — today / this week / this month 
• Total revenue and commission earned — today / this week / this month 
• Pending bookings (not yet assigned to a driver) 
• New owner/driver registration requests (pending approval) 
• Active vs. completed vs. cancelled booking counts 
• Quick alerts: expiring fitness certificates/tax tokens, expiring subscriptions 
Panel 2 — Owner Registration Panel  
Two-layer verification: the business/company is approved first, then each car under it is verified and approved 
separately.  
2A. Registration Queue  
• List of all registration submissions — filter by Pending / Approved / Rejected 
• Filter by business type (Individual owner / Rental agency), area, and submission date 
• Quick search by owner name or phone number

2D. Driver Review  
• Driver list with NID, driving license (front-back), photo, years of experience 
• Which car each driver is currently assigned to 
• Individually approve, suspend, or block a driver 
• In MVP, driver verification can be optional — owner-and-car verification is enough to launch
