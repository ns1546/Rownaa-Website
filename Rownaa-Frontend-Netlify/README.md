# ROWNAA® Luxury Chauffeur & Fleet Platform — Full Website Netlify Package

This folder contains the complete, production-ready static deployment package for the entire **Rownaa® Bangladesh** web platform.

## 🚀 How to Deploy on Netlify (Drag & Drop)

1. Open **[Netlify Drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop)** in your web browser.
2. Sign in to your Netlify account.
3. Drag and drop this entire `Rownaa-Frontend-Netlify` folder into the drop area.
4. Netlify will deploy your full website in seconds with a free SSL certificate!

---

## 📂 Included Pages & Portals

* **`/` or `/index.html`** — Main Rownaa Brand Homepage (Services, Fleet Showroom, Booking CTA)
* **`/driver-register`** — Dedicated Chauffeur & Rental Fleet Registration Portal (v7.0 with live Google Sheet integration)
* **`/find-cars`** — Luxury Car Fleet Finder & Reservation Search
* **`/customer-panel`** — Passenger VIP Account & Booking Management
* **`/owner-panel`** — Car Owner & Fleet Partner Operations Portal
* **`/owner-register`** — Vehicle Owner Onboarding Form
* **`/admin-panel`** — Executive Operations Control Center
* **`/checkout`** — Chauffeur Trip Booking & Payment Confirmation
* **`/login`** — Sliding Authentication Panel
